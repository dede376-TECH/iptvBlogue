faq:
  - question: 'Why does IPTV buffer when my internet speed test is fast?'
    answer: 'A speed test measures a short burst to a nearby server. Live streams need a steady, uninterrupted rate for hours, often from a distant server. Wi-Fi retries, DNS detours and a small player buffer cause stalls even on fast lines.'
  - question: 'Does a VPN stop IPTV buffering?'
    answer: 'Sometimes, if your ISP routes badly to the streaming server, and sometimes it makes things worse by adding another hop. Test with and without, using the same stream at the same time of day.'
