## Scope

IPTV Blogue publishes educational and informational content only. We do not sell products or
subscriptions, and we do not accept payment for coverage of any service.

## Standards every article must meet

1. **Hands-on where it matters.** Setup and troubleshooting guides are tested on the device or
   app described, and include the writer's own screenshots or configuration examples.
2. **No invented numbers.** Statistics, prices, channel counts and quotes are either cited to a
   named source or omitted. Placeholders are never published.
3. **Legal sources only.** We never link to playlists, resellers or services we cannot verify
   as licensed, and we never describe a service as licensed without evidence.
4. **Clear structure.** One H1, logical headings, a table of contents, published and updated
   dates, and a named author with a real biography.
5. **Software-assisted, human-edited.** Outlines and structure may be drafted with software,
   but a person edits, tests and signs off every article.

## Updates and corrections

Articles show their last updated date. We review guides when the apps or devices they cover
change. If you find an error, use the [contact page](/contact/); confirmed corrections are
applied and noted in the article.

## Advertising

Advertising is served by third-party networks and clearly separated from content. Ads never
influence what we cover or how we rate anything. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) and
[cookie policy](/cookies/) for how ad personalisation is controlled.
