## Scope

Screenwise publishes educational and informational content only. We do not sell products or subscriptions, and we do not accept payment for coverage of any service.

## Standards every article must meet

1. **No invented numbers.** Statistics, prices, channel counts and quotes are either cited to a named source or omitted. Points that still need checking are kept as hidden review notes in the source, not in the visible text.
2. **No unearned test claims.** A setup or troubleshooting step is described as tested only when it was actually tested, with the writer's own screenshots or configuration examples.
3. **Legal sources only.** We never link to playlists, resellers or services we cannot verify as licensed, and we never describe a service as licensed without evidence.
4. **Clear structure.** One H1, logical headings, a table of contents, published and updated dates, and a byline that matches a published author page. That page states only facts that have been provided.
5. **AI-assisted drafting, visible review status.** Guides are drafted with AI assistance. Each guide shows its review status under the byline. A guide is marked approved only after hands-on testing on a real device and a human review. Until then it says "AI-assisted guide, not yet tested on a device", and it carries no step-by-step or FAQ structured data.

## Updates and corrections

Articles show their publication date, and an updated date when the text has changed since. If you find an error, use the [contact page](/contact/). The form does not deliver messages until a role mailbox exists. When a published guide is corrected, the change is listed with its date in a Corrections section at the end of that guide.

## Images

- **Screenshots** come only from hands-on testing on the device named in the guide. They are never generated, and a guide shows screenshots only once its tested devices are listed.
- **Illustrations** may be made with AI tools. Every illustration carries a visible "Illustration" label.
- **Diagrams** are drawn for the guide and describe only what the text says.
- No logos, brand marks or recognisable people appear in images.

## Advertising

Advertising is switched off while the host does not allow commercial use. If it is turned on later, it will be labelled, separated from articles, and it will not decide what we cover. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) and [cookie policy](/cookies/).
