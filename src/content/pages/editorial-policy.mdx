## Scope

Screenwise publishes educational and informational content only. We do not sell products or subscriptions, and we do not accept payment for coverage of any service.

## Standards every article must meet

1. **No invented numbers.** Statistics, prices, channel counts and quotes are either cited to a named source or omitted. Unfinished notes are never left in a published article.
2. **No unearned test claims.** A setup or troubleshooting step is described as tested only when it was actually tested, with the writer's own screenshots or configuration examples.
3. **Legal sources only.** We never link to playlists, resellers or services we cannot verify as licensed, and we never describe a service as licensed without evidence.
4. **Clear structure.** One H1, logical headings, a table of contents, published and updated dates, and a byline that matches a published author page. That page states only facts that have been provided.
5. **Software-assisted, human-edited.** Outlines and structure may be drafted with software, but a person edits and signs off every article.

## Updates and corrections

Articles show their last updated date. We review guides when the apps or devices they cover change. If you find an error, use the [contact page](/contact/). The form does not deliver messages until a role mailbox exists. Confirmed corrections are applied and noted in the article.

## Advertising

Advertising is switched off while the host does not allow commercial use. If it is turned on later, it will be labelled, separated from articles, and it will not decide what we cover. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) and [cookie policy](/cookies/).
