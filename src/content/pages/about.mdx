IPTV Blogue is an independent, informational site about IPTV technology for readers in the
United Kingdom and the United States. We explain how playlists, programme guides and players
work, how to set them up on common devices, how to fix the usual problems, and what the law
actually says about streaming.

## What we do not do

- We do **not** sell or resell IPTV subscriptions.
- We do **not** link to playlists, channel lists, resellers or any unlicensed service.
- We do **not** publish invented statistics, reviews, prices or channel counts. Where a number
  matters, we cite the source or test it ourselves.

## How articles are produced

Outlines may be drafted with software assistance, but every published article is edited by a
person, includes original material (screenshots, configuration examples or test notes) and
follows our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/). Corrections are welcome via the
[contact page](/contact/).
