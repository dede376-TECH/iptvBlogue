Screenwise publishes plain-English guides for everyday viewers. The guides explain how IPTV playlists, programme guides and players work, how setup is usually done, how to approach common problems, and what to check before treating a streaming service as lawful.

Who operates the site beyond the name Screenwise, and any postal address, are not published.

## What we do not do

- We do **not** sell or resell IPTV subscriptions.
- We do **not** link to playlists, channel lists, resellers or any unlicensed service.
- We do **not** publish invented statistics, reviews, prices or channel counts. Where a number matters, we cite a source. We do not claim to have tested something we have not tested.

## How articles are produced

Outlines may be drafted with software assistance, but a published article is edited by a person and follows the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/). Corrections go through the [contact page](/contact/). The contact form does not deliver messages yet.
