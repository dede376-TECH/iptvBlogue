The controls under this page show only the categories that are actually switched on. While analytics and advertising are off, there is nothing to accept and this site sets no optional cookies.

## Categories

- **Strictly necessary.** Used only to remember a cookie choice, and only once an optional category is switched on. It cannot be switched off while that choice is stored.
- **Analytics.** Google Analytics 4, to see which guides are read and how far people scroll. Loaded only after you accept, and only if analytics is switched on. Off by default.
- **Advertising.** Cookies set by an advertising partner to personalise and measure ads. Loaded only after you accept, and only if advertising is switched on. Off by default, including while the host does not allow commercial use. Declining is also the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" choice. Ads are not shown without that consent.

## Consent Mode

When analytics or advertising is switched on, the site uses Google Consent Mode v2. Until you choose, optional storage is denied and those tags do not run. While both are switched off, no consent tag is loaded.

## Managing cookies in your browser

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the strictly necessary entry means a banner, if one is in use, will appear again on the next visit.
