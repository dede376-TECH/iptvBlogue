Use the controls below to change your consent at any time. Your choice is stored in your
browser only (no account needed) and applies to this site.

## Categories

- **Strictly necessary.** Remembering your cookie choice. Cannot be switched off.
- **Analytics.** Google Analytics 4, used to see which guides are read and how far people
  scroll. Off by default.
- **Advertising.** Cookies set by our advertising partners to personalise and measure ads. Off
  by default. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

## Consent Mode

We use Google Consent Mode v2. Until you make a choice, all optional storage is denied and no
analytics or advertising tags run. Your choice is sent to those tags only after you confirm it.

## Managing cookies in your browser

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the strictly necessary
cookie means the banner will appear again on your next visit.
