## What we collect

- **Server logs.** Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes IP addresses and request data to
  serve the site and protect it from abuse. Logs are retained for a limited period.
- **Analytics (only with consent).** If you accept analytics cookies we use Google Analytics 4
  to understand which articles are read and how far people scroll. IP addresses are not stored
  by GA4, and we do not use analytics for advertising.
- **Advertising (only with consent).** If you accept advertising cookies, our advertising
  partners may set cookies to personalise ads and measure them. Without consent, ads are shown
  without personalisation.
- **Email.** If you contact us we keep your message to reply and for our records.

## Legal basis

Essential processing is based on our legitimate interest in running a secure website. Analytics
and advertising cookies are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time on the
[cookie preferences page](/cookies/).

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR / EU GDPR you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability
of your data, and object to processing. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA,
including the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; use the
**Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information** link in the footer to opt out of advertising
cookies.
