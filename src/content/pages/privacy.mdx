## Who we are

The publisher of this site is **Screenwise**. No personal name, postal address, registration number or country of establishment is published, because those details have not been provided.

There is no email address yet. A generic role mailbox will be added when the site has its own domain. Until then, the [contact page](/contact/) does not send messages.

## What we collect

- **Hosting logs.** The host (Vercel, temporary) processes connection data, including IP addresses, to deliver the site. This page does not state a retention period, because the host's retention for this project has not been confirmed.
- **Analytics.** Google Analytics 4 is not loaded unless analytics is switched on for the site and you accept analytics cookies. It is switched off by default. This page does not describe Google's IP or retention settings; those have to be confirmed before analytics is switched on.
- **Advertising.** No ads are shown and no advertising cookies are set unless advertising is switched on and you accept advertising cookies. Advertising is switched off while the host does not allow commercial use. Declining advertising cookies means advertising tags are not loaded. That decline is also the opt-out of sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context advertising.
- **Messages.** No mailbox is published, so the site does not receive email today. If a role mailbox is added later, messages sent to it would be kept long enough to reply and to record corrections. How long has not been decided.

## Legal basis

Essential processing is based on the legitimate interest in running a secure website. Analytics and advertising cookies, once those tools are switched on, are based on your consent, which you can withdraw on the [cookie preferences page](/cookies/).

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and object to processing. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. Use the **Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information** link in the footer. While advertising is switched off, that opt-out is already in effect.

## Third parties

Hosting: Vercel (temporary). Analytics, if switched on later: Google Analytics 4. Advertising, if switched on later: Google AdSense. Legal names and addresses of those companies are not copied here.
